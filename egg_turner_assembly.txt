# 🛠 Egg Turner Assembly Guide (Stepper Motor System)
This guide explains how to assemble and integrate the egg turner system using **one NEMA 23 stepper motor per vertical stack**, ensuring trays tilt **30-45 degrees in either direction**.

---

## **1️⃣ Required Components**
✅ **NEMA 23 Stepper Motor** (1 per vertical stack)
✅ **TB6600 Stepper Driver**
✅ **24V DC Power Supply**
✅ **Mounting Bracket for Stepper Motor**
✅ **Vertical Rod or Pivoting Linkage System**
✅ **Egg Trays with Adjustable Tilt Points**
✅ **Screws & Fasteners**

---

## **2️⃣ Assembly Instructions**
### **A) Mechanical Setup**
1️⃣ **Mount the Stepper Motor:**
   - Secure the **NEMA 23 stepper motor** onto the incubator frame using screws or fasteners.
   - Ensure the motor axis is positioned at **the center of the vertical stack** to allow smooth movement.
   - Attach a **pivoting linkage or vertical rod** that connects to **all trays** in the stack.

2️⃣ **Connect the Vertical Rod System:**
   - The motor moves **a single metal or nylon rod**, which **tilts all trays in sync**.
   - **Each tray should pivot on a fixed hinge** to allow movement **without excessive resistance**.
   - **Lubricate pivot points if necessary** to ensure frictionless movement.

3️⃣ **Adjust Movement Range:**
   - **Stepper Motor:** Can rotate **precisely to any angle**, allowing **custom tilt adjustments**.
   - **Tilt trays to 30-45° over XX seconds**.
   - **Return to level position for tray removal**.

---

## **3️⃣ Troubleshooting Guide**
| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| **Trays not tilting smoothly** | Vertical rod resistance | Lubricate hinge points or adjust linkage tension |
| **Motor not responding** | Wiring issue | Check GPIO & power connections |
| **Stepper motor skipping steps** | Low torque setting | Increase stepper driver current |
