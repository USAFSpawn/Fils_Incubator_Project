# 🛠 Egg Turner Assembly Guide

## 🔹 Required Components:
- MG995 Servo Motor
- 12V Power Supply
- PWM Controller (GPIO18)

## 🔹 Assembly Instructions:
1️⃣ Mount the servo motor securely inside the incubator.
2️⃣ Connect the **PWM signal** to Raspberry Pi GPIO18.
3️⃣ Attach egg trays to the servo motor axis.
4️⃣ Verify movement using `test_motors.py`.

✅ Your egg turner is now functional!
